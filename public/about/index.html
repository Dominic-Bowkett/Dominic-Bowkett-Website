The practice.

Independent building surveyor, domestic energy assessor and retrofit assessor. Hartfield, East Sussex. Practitioner-led, plain-English, fixed fees.

Hello, I’m Dom.

I run an independent surveying practice from Hartfield, East Sussex, working with homeowners, landlords and small developers across Kent, Sussex, Surrey and London.

The practice has one full-time surveyor — me. Every report you receive is one I’ve walked, measured, written and signed. There is no junior, no template-and-tweak, no white-label sub-contracting. I’m an Associate Member of the Chartered Association of Building Engineers (ACABE). Email is the best way to reach me; if you ring, your call is answered, your details taken, and I’ll ring you back the same day.

What you’ll get

  • Plain-English reports. No jargon for its own sake. If a recommendation needs technical language, it’ll be there — alongside a translation.
  • A fixed fee, agreed up front. No sliding scale, no “extras” on completion. You’ll know what it costs before I instruct.
  • A working relationship, not a transaction. Most clients ring after the report to talk through it. That call is included.
  • Independence. No introducer fees, no kickbacks from contractors, no recommended-builder list with hidden incentives.
  • Fully insured and DBS checked. Professional indemnity cover is in place and I’m DBS checked — peace of mind when I’m in your home.

How I work

Site visits are typically two to four hours for a domestic property. I take a long look at the structure, the envelope, the services, the drainage and the immediate ground; photograph what matters; and check the things that often get missed because they’re uncomfortable to access.

The report follows within five working days for most surveys. Energy assessments are quicker — the certificate is usually lodged the same day as the visit. Retrofit assessments take longer, because the analysis is deeper and the deliverable feeds into a wider design process.

If a property is unusual — listed, traditionally built, off-grid, recently extended, or has a quirky history — that’s the kind of work I most enjoy.

Coverage

The practice covers Kent, Sussex, Surrey and London. Postcode areas regularly worked: TN, RH, ME, DA, BR, SE, E, RM, KT, CR, SM, BN. If you’re outside that radius and the job is right, get in touch — I’ll travel.
