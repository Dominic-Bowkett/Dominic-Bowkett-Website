Retrofit surveys,
for your clients.

For building surveyors whose clients are buying, or have just bought, and are thinking about making the house warmer and cheaper to run. Refer them to me for a retrofit survey and I’ll pay you 20% of the fee.

Why your clients need one

Your survey tells the buyer what’s wrong with the house. The next question is usually “what should we do to it?” Insulation, new windows, a heat pump, solar: done in the wrong order, or without dealing with damp and ventilation first, energy upgrades can cause condensation, mould and wasted money.

A retrofit survey answers that question for the particular house. It sets out which upgrades make sense, what each one will do to the building, and the order to do them in.

Who does the work

I do every survey myself. I’m an accredited Retrofit Assessor, a Domestic Energy Assessor and a building surveyor reporting in the RPSA format, so I look at the fabric, the energy model and the risks together rather than one at a time.

What the survey covers

  • Your defects report as the starting point. I don’t repeat your condition survey. I work from your findings, and with your permission I’ll use your photos too, so the client isn’t paying twice.
  • Energy performance. A full energy assessment of the house as it is, including whether the current EPC is right and how it compares with the current methodology.
  • Ventilation. I test or check the existing ventilation and say what’s needed before the house is made more airtight.
  • Occupancy and plans. Who will live there, how they’ll use the house now, and what they plan to do with it later.
  • Thermal imaging and, where it helps, drone survey of roofs and high-level details.
  • A plan for the house. A written report with upgrades in a sensible order over the short, medium and long term.

I follow PAS 2035 principles throughout. This is a private survey for the homeowner rather than a lodged PAS 2035 assessment, so it suits people planning their own works. If the client later needs a lodged assessment for a funding scheme, I can do that too.

Fees and commission

Prices start from £500 + VAT. Larger or unusual houses are priced on floor area: once I have the address and your report I’ll give a fixed quote, usually the same day.

You receive 20% of my fee, excluding VAT, for every survey you refer that goes ahead, paid once the client has paid.
