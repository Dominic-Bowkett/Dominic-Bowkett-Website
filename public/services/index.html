Services.

Four lines of work, all delivered personally. Pick the one you need and click through for scope, deliverables, timelines and what to expect.

01 / Building surveys

Building
surveys.

RPSA surveys for buyers, sellers, owners and landlords — Home Buyer/Condition, Building, Buy-to-Let and new-build snagging. Roof to drains, plain English, illustrated. The right survey for the property in front of you — not the most expensive one.

Read → 02 / EPCs

Energy
certificates.

Domestic Energy Performance Certificates lodged on the central register. Required for sale, let and most lender applications. Certificates usually ready the same day as the visit.

Read → 03 / Retrofit

Retrofit
assessments.

PAS 2035-compliant assessments for ECO grants, MEES exemptions and self-funded retrofit. Fabric-first, building-specific, written so the design team and the homeowner can both work from it.

Read → 04 / Ventilation

Ventilation
testing.

Domestic background ventilation testing, carried out as part of retrofit. Intermittent, MEV, MVHR and passive-stack systems measured with a Testo 417 anemometer — so airflow keeps pace with the fabric work. Not a Building Control compliance service.
