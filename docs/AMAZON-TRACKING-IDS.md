# Amazon tracking IDs per guide

Each guide below has its own Amazon Associates tracking ID, so Associates Central
(Reports > Summary, Group by: Tracking Id) shows clicks, orders and earnings per guide.
`scripts/add-amazon-tracking.py` (run by rebuild-journal.py) swaps the guide's links
from opeconltd-21 to its own tag. Guides not listed keep opeconltd-21.

The tag MUST exist in Associates Central (Account > Manage Tracking IDs) before it is
added here, or clicks earn nothing. The account allows 100 tracking IDs; 17 were in use
before these. Created 4 Nov 2026 for the top Field Kit guides by Google clicks
(Aug-Nov 2026), plus damp meters.
